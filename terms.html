Daybloom Rules and Terms

Last updated October 9, 2026

The short version: Daybloom is for planning your days and staying close to your friends. Be kind. There is zero tolerance for bullying, hateful, sexual, or dangerous content, or for people who post it. If anyone makes you uncomfortable, block them and report them.

Community rules

In chats, names, group names, photos, and events you share, do not:

  • Bully, threaten, harass, or embarrass anyone.
  • Post hateful content about anyone's race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, or background.
  • Share sexual or nude content of anyone, ever.
  • Post violence, self-harm, or anything dangerous or illegal.
  • Share someone else's private information, like their address, phone number, or passwords.
  • Pretend to be someone else, send spam, or try to break or misuse the app.

Daybloom automatically hides many swear words and slurs, but that filter is not perfect. You are responsible for what you post.

Staying safe

  • Only add friends you know in real life. Friend codes change every 45 seconds so they are shared in person.
  • Block: open a friend or a chat and choose Block. They are removed as a friend, cannot add you again, and cannot message you. Their messages are hidden in group chats. You can unblock in Settings.
  • Report: press and hold a message (or right-click on a computer) and choose Report, or open a friend and choose Report. Reports go to the people who run Daybloom, who review them as soon as possible and remove content or accounts that break these rules.
  • Events marked Only me are never shown to friends.
  • If you are ever in danger, talk to a parent, a trusted adult, or call your local emergency number.

Who can use Daybloom

You must be at least 13 years old to use Daybloom. If you are under the age of adulthood where you live, a parent or guardian should review these terms with you.

Your account

  • Keep your password private. Daybloom accounts use a username and password, so a lost password cannot be recovered by email.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your profile, events, friends, and the messages and photos you sent.
  • We may remove content, or suspend or delete accounts, that break these rules.

The app itself

Daybloom is provided as is. We work to keep it running and safe, but we cannot promise it will always be available or free of mistakes, and we are not responsible for missed events or reminders. Reminders depend on your device's notification settings.

We may update these rules and terms. If something important changes, the app will ask you to agree again.

Contact and support

For help, questions, or problems, open Daybloom and go to Settings → Help and safety → Contact support. Your message goes straight to the people who run Daybloom. To report a person or a message, use Report inside the app.
