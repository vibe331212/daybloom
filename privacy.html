Daybloom Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

The short version: Daybloom only keeps what it needs to run your calendar, your friends, and your chats. There are no ads, no tracking, and your information is never sold. Daybloom never asks for your email, phone number, location, or contacts. You can delete your account and your data at any time inside the app.

What stays on your device

Your mode, screen shade, ringtone, reminder settings, stopwatch history, and world clocks are saved only on your device. A copy of your events is also kept on your device so the calendar works without internet. In the iPhone app, upcoming reminders and timers are handed to your iPhone's notifications so they can go off when the app is closed.

What Daybloom does not collect

  • No email address, phone number, real name, or birthday.
  • No location, contacts, health data, or microphone recordings.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, and no analytics that follow you across apps or websites.
  • The camera and photo library are only used when you choose a photo to send or use as a group photo.

Services that help run Daybloom

  • Supabase stores accounts, events, friends, chats, and photos on servers in the United States, and handles sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Daybloom website.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and cdnjs deliver the app's font and a few code libraries. Like any website, they can see your device's internet address when these load, but they do not receive your Daybloom information.

These services only process information so Daybloom can work. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

Keeping your information safe

Everything is sent over encrypted connections. The database only lets each person read what they're allowed to see: your own information, your friends' shared events, and the chats you're in. Private chat photos can only be opened by people in that chat. No system is perfect, so keep your password private.

How long information is kept

  • Your information is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete) erases your sign-in, profile, events, friends, blocks, and the messages, photos, and reactions you sent, and clears Daybloom's data on that device. Group chats stay for the other members, without your messages.
  • Reports are kept so the people who run Daybloom can keep everyone safe. When you delete your account, reports you sent no longer show who sent them.

Children

Daybloom is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us through the app and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Mark events Only me to keep them from friends.
  • Remove friends, leave group chats, or block people at any time.
  • Turn notifications on or off in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Delete your account inside the app. To ask for a copy of your information, contact support in the app.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update this page and let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open Daybloom and go to Settings → Help and safety → Contact support. See also the Community Rules and Terms.
