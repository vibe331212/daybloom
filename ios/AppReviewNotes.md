# Notes for Apple App Review

Paste the text below into App Store Connect → your app → App Review Information → Notes.
Use username `appreview` and the password saved in `review-account.local.txt` (that file stays on your Mac).

---

Thank you for reviewing Daybloom, a calendar, clock, and private messaging app for friends.

Sign in on the first screen with the demo account above (Sign in tab). It already has:
- Events on the calendar, including repeating, all-day, and one private ("Only me") event.
- Two friends, Maya and Leo (demo accounts we control), whose shared schedules appear under Friends.
- A one-on-one chat with Maya and a group chat called "Study group".

Things to try:
- Calendar: tap New event, turn on All day or set a reminder. Reminders and timers use local notifications, so they go off even when the app is closed. The app asks for notification permission the first time you set one.
- Clock: world clock, timer, stopwatch, and 300 built-in ringtones.
- Friends → Chats: send a message or photo; double-tap or press and hold a message to react, copy, or report it.

Safety features (Guideline 1.2):
- Users must accept the Community Rules (zero tolerance for objectionable content) when signing up; existing users accept them once.
- Report: press and hold any message → Report, or open a friend / a chat's ⋯ menu / a group member → Report. Reports go to the developer, who reviews them.
- Block: same places → Block. Blocking removes the friend, prevents new requests and messages, and hides their group messages. Manage in Settings → Help and safety.
- A server-side word filter masks profanity and slurs in messages, names, and group names.
- Friends can only be added in person with a code that changes every 45 seconds.

Account deletion (Guideline 5.1.1(v)): Settings → Account → Delete. If you delete the demo account, please let us know and we will recreate it.

The app does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics, and does not collect email, phone numbers, location, or contacts.
